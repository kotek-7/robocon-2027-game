# Product

## Platform
web

## Stack
Godot 4.7, GDScript, Compatibility renderer. PC browser, keyboard/mouse and gamepad.

## Users
Students learning the 2027 competition through short playable matches.

## Product purpose
Drive TR and BR, transport blocks, build and contest towers, and race for Mustika in three-minute matches.

## Capabilities
VS CPU, automatic casual online matchmaking, unlimited free practice. Both robots are manually controlled, with one active driver per team. Fixed identical performance. Minimal text and no configuration or analysis workspace.

## Brand commitments
Vivid red and blue robots, golden-yellow platforms, cyan surroundings and purple barriers. Rounded forms and playful motion retain the toy character. Fall Guys is the reference for warmth and physical animation. The field preserves supplied CAD dimensions.

## Evidence
Local English rulebook, Japanese reference translation, block appendix and field STEP. Terminology and rule interpretations are shared with the neighboring gakusei-robocon project.
